About Us

Gildia is an online platform built for conference and event teams who need official graphics fast. We help organizers create invitations, badges, programs, banners, and social media assets with confidence.

Our mission is to make event design accessible for government, corporate, and international meeting projects. Whether you're planning a summit, ceremony, or public forum, Gildia provides clean templates and easy tools to launch polished event materials quickly.

We focus on three core principles:

  • Simplicity: Intuitive design tools for non-designers
  • Quality: Official visuals that work in print and online
  • Speed: Ready-made templates for fast event preparation

Join event managers and organizers who trust Gildia for professional conference and meeting graphics. Start designing today and bring your event identity to life.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Gildia's services.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our services effectively. This includes:

  • Account information (email address, name) when you create an account
  • Event asset data that you create and store on our platform
  • Usage data to improve our services and user experience

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our event graphics creation and management services
  • Process your requests and transactions
  • Send you important updates about our services
  • Improve our platform and develop new features
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our services

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored securely and is never sold to third parties. We use encryption and secure protocols to safeguard your data during transmission and storage.

Cookies

We use cookies only for essential site functionality, such as maintaining your session and remembering your preferences. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Third-Party Services

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide our services (such as cloud hosting providers) or as required by law. All third-party service providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at support@gildia.com.
